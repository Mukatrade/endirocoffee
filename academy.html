Skills for people of all abilities.

We train, hire and support people of all abilities in all our cafés. The Endiro Academy in Nakasero is where the training starts.

Why inclusion.

In Uganda, people with disabilities often meet stigma. It keeps them from school, health care, formal work and community life.

8%of people with disabilities in Uganda have a formal job.

We changed our cafés and the way we hire, so that our staff with disabilities get the same chances to work and to grow.

From one café to every café.

The Sign Language Café opened in Nakasero in 2020. There we began to hire on purpose within the deaf community, and other businesses in Uganda changed how they hire too.

One café was not enough. Now staff with disabilities work across all our cafés, and the team from the Sign Language Café moved to other locations.
