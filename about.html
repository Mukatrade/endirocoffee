Coffee that ends child vulnerability.

Endiro started in Kampala in 2011. Our vision is to end child vulnerability globally through coffee, its people, its profits and its spaces.

Brew all the good we can.

We believe in hospitality, conversation, prayer, partnership, community and family. That is where good coffee that does good things comes from.

Since 2011 Endiro has supported projects for vulnerable children and families. In 2014 we started to change how we do the coffee business, at every step, so that all of it has a good effect on people.

In 2015 we met coffee farmers in Bukalasi. Their coffee had a Fair Trade label, but middlemen took the money. So we formed our own farmer groups, paid them better prices, and gave them training and tools.

Our mission, 2016 to 2026.

Four parts, one aim: the vision above.

1. Tree to cup

Run every step of the coffee chain, from the coffee tree to the coffee cup.

2. Quality

Make every step better and bigger, so that more coffee reaches the highest standard.

3. Growth

Open more cafés in Uganda and abroad, sell in shops and supermarkets, and supply green and roasted beans.

4. Community

Work with the people where we grow, process and sell coffee: mentoring, jobs and education for the most vulnerable.

The founders.

Gloria Katusiime and Cody Lorance met at the Lausanne Congress in Cape Town in 2010. Neither had a business background: Gloria was a journalist, Cody a pastor.

Gloria KatusiimeFounder and Co-CEO. From Uganda, a journalist before Endiro.

Partners and recognition.

B Corp checks the whole business: farmer pay, organic and sustainable farming, renewable energy on the farms, and pay inside the company (the highest pay is not more than 10 times the lowest). B Corp also certifies Endiro as majority black-owned and woman-owned.

Certified B Corp

Certified in October 2019. Overall B Impact Score 107.3 (the median for ordinary businesses is 50.9).
