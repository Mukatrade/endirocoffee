Questions and answers.

About the coffee, the farming and how to order.

Where is your coffee from?

Our flagship coffee grows in Bukalasi village, Bududa District, in the Mt. Elgon range. Farmer groups grow it, most of them women who own their land. They earn some of the highest pay of any coffee farmers in Uganda, and they have built schools with that income.

What does it taste like?

Medium body, mild acidity and a sweet, fruity finish. Notes of dried cranberry, walnut and allspice.

Which roast do you offer?

By colour and oil it is near a medium roast. We do not print that on the bag. We roast each bean for the profile its origin gives, not for a colour.

Which brew method do you recommend?

All of them work. In our cafés we serve espresso and house coffee, plus pour-over, Aeropress, French press and jerry can cold brew. Pour-over and Aeropress bring out the fruity notes best.

Is the coffee organic?

It is farmed organically, but it is not certified organic. The farmers use no synthetic fertilizer or pesticide. Water is from springs, waterfalls and rain tanks. Electricity on the farms is solar.

Is it fair trade?

We pay above the Fair Trade floor price. We chose to be a Certified B Corp instead of single-issue labels, because B Corp checks farmer pay, farming, energy, water and pay inside the company together.
